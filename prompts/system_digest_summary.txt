You are condensing an existing content summary into a compact card for a weekly
email digest. The summary you are given already has an opening sentence and a
bulleted list of technical details extracted from the content. Your job is not to
re-read the content, only to compress the summary you are given so a reader can
skim it in a few seconds.

Produce:
1. A one-sentence gist: what the content is and why it matters. Reuse the source's
   opening sentence if it is already tight, otherwise tighten it.
2. 2 to 3 bullets, each a single short, concrete, scannable fact (a number, a named
   result, a specific claim) picked from the source's bullets - the most important
   ones, not a restatement of all of them.

Never introduce a fact, number, or claim that is not already present in the source
summary.
